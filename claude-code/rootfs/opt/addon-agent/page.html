Set up Telegram

Step 1 of 5 · in Telegram

Make your bot with @BotFather

Telegram only makes bots in its own app, so this step is yours. It takes about a minute.

  1. Open @BotFather ↗
  2. Send /newbot
  3. Give it a name, for example Home helper.
  4. Give it a username ending in bot, for example my_home_helper_bot.
  5. BotFather answers with a token (a long line with a colon). Copy it and come back here.

Don't send the token to anyone or paste it into a chat, including a chat with Claude. It goes only in the next step.

Step 2 of 5 · here

Paste the token

Checked with Telegram at once. Kept in this app's own folder, never shown again or written to the log. Its commands (/status, /routines, /run, /stop) and description are filled in for you.

Step 3 of 5 · one tap in Telegram

Connect your chat

Open your bot and press Start. The link carries a one-time code, so only your press connects. It works for 10 minutes.

Step 4 of 5

Test message

Sent: "Hello from AI Intervention. This is where I'll tell you about your home." Did it arrive?

Still nothing? Check that you pressed Start in the right bot, then go back a step.

Step 5 of 5 · optional

Let Home Assistant use this bot

Adds Home Assistant's own Telegram bot integration, send-only, for your chat. Messages you send the bot that aren't for Claude arrive as telegram_command and telegram_text events. Home Assistant keeps its own copy of the token for this.
